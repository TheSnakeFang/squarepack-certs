# Six, eight or ten unit squares in a strip of height just below 2: the minimum width is F_n(H) (v0.3, 2026-11-01)

Status: v0.3, 2026-11-01, archived as https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23865706 (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23852983; the files there are this text without the DOI line, plus the four O2 leaf bundles). This version extends v0.2 (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23852984, six squares on 1.87 ≤ H < 2) to n ∈ {6, 8, 10} and lowers the n = 6 range. Every mathematical step is either in the repository https://github.com/TheSnakeFang/squarepack with a blind referee report, or is a certified computation with a replay command; raw computational provenance for v0.2 is in https://github.com/TheSnakeFang/squarepack-certs (`strip-n6/`), and for this version in `strip-even/` there (the O2 leaf bundles, four tar files of 4.0 GB in all, are too large for GitHub and are deposited as files of the Zenodo record of this version; `strip-even/o2n-cells.sha256` lists their SHA-256).

Notation: n = 2m, k = 2 − H, w = x + 1/x, D(x) = 1 + w − √(w(w + 2)), d0 = 3 − 2√2, and

    f_n(T; H) = n + k(mT + (m − 1)/T) − (n − 2) D(T),   F_n(H) = min_{T > 0} f_n(T; H).

The minimum is attained at a unique T_n(H) ∈ (√((m − 1)/m), 1] (`o2n/LEMMAS.md` Lemma 6.3); F_n is strictly decreasing, F_n(2) = n − (n − 2)(3 − 2√2), and H\*(n) denotes the unique height with F_n(H\*(n)) = n: H\*(6) = 1.8610971, H\*(8) = 1.8520484, H\*(10) = 1.8469355 (Lemma 6.3(e); the digits are numerical and are not proof inputs). For n = 6, F_6 agrees with the F6 = min_θ L(θ, H) of v0.2 to 1e-15 at H = 1.87, 1.9, 1.95 (LEMMAS.md §0). That identity is numerical and the proof does not use it (see Assembly in §1). Reference values: F_8(1.9) = 7.6670536343, F_10(1.9) = 9.5247406413 (`o1n/SPEC.md`).

**Theorem.** Let n ∈ {6, 8, 10} and H ∈ R_n, where R_6 = [1.862, 2), R_8 = [1.855, 2), R_10 = [1.850, 2). Every packing of n unit squares (arbitrary rotations, pairwise disjoint interiors) in a rectangle of width L and height H has L ≥ F_n(H).

F_n(H) is the Morandi width: the parametric boundary piece in Erich Friedman's Math Magic problem of June 2010 (https://erich-friedman.github.io/mathmagic/0610.html, credited there to Maurizio Morandi), realised by two upright squares in opposite corners and n − 2 squares of a common tilt φ between them, alternately on the floor and the ceiling, i.e. the chain Φ_M at tilts (0, φ, …, φ, 0). For n = 6 the configuration was checked to be a packing of width F_6(H) in exact rational arithmetic (`runs/openmath-2026-10-27/proof-F6.md` §3.1), so F_6(H) is the minimum width on R_6. For n = 8, 10 the equal-tilt chain agrees numerically with F_n (`o1n/SPEC.md`), but no exact check that the configuration is a packing is cited here, so for n = 8, 10 sharpness rests on Friedman's page while the lower bound is proved. The lower bound does not use the construction.

Each R_n is the intersection of the O1 range, which starts at 1.862, 1.855, 1.850, and the O2 range [H_lo(n), 2] with the rationals H_lo(n) = 1.861098, 1.852049, 1.846936 just above H\*(n) (the rounded 1.861097 and 1.852048 lie below the true roots). Below H\*(n) the row of n upright squares has width n < F_n(H), so L ≥ F_n(H) is false there; [H\*(n), lower end of R_n) is open for each n. For n = 6, v0.2 proved [1.87, 2); a new O1 run adds [1.862, 1.87] and the new O2 computation covers [1.861098, 2], so R_6 = [1.862, 2). H = 2 is excluded for the reason given in v0.2 (§4).

## 1. Structure of the proof

1. **Reduction to chain functions** (`docs/strip-n6-reduction.md`, refereed: `docs/strip-n6-reduction-review-codex-2026-10-31.md`; its n-independence confirmed in finding 4 of `docs/o1n-review-codex-2026-11-01.md`). As in v0.2 (sort centres by x, pair constraint κ for consecutive squares, concavity in the heights), using only 2h(θ) ≤ √2 < H < 2 and never n: L ≥ Φ^σ(θ, H) for the packing's tilts θ and some role pattern σ ∈ {F, C}^n, where Φ^σ = h(θ_1) + h(θ_n) + Σ_{i=1}^{n−1} κ(θ_i, θ_{i+1}, dy_i). The mirror pattern CFCF… reduces to FCFC… by reflecting the strip.
2. **O1, the 2^n − 2 non-alternating patterns** (`o1n/`, refereed for n = 8, 10: `docs/o1n-review-codex-2026-11-01.md`; n = 6 on [1.87, 2) is v0.2's `o1dp/`). For n = 6 on [1.862, 1.87], n = 8 on [1.855, 2) and n = 10 on [1.850, 2), every non-alternating σ has Φ^σ(θ, H) > F_n_ub ≥ Φ_M(θ0, H) for all θ ∈ [0, π/2]^n. This is the certified min-plus DP of v0.2 with the chain length as a parameter: the DP runs over chain states, so the number of patterns costs nothing, and the three gap lower bounds (plain interval, correlated ratio, near-upright pair lemma) are byte-identical to v0.2's kernel. One change was needed: the first o1n draft bounded the target on an H-slice through monotonicity, justified by a claim that κ is even in dy, which is false (`o1n/math-check.py` has the counterexample). The target is now an interval-in-H evaluation of the Morandi chain at one fixed tilt vector θ0 = (0, φ0, …, φ0, 0), an upper bound F_n_ub for Φ_M(θ0, H) on the whole slice, with no monotonicity assumed. Slices: 16 (n = 6 gap), 145 (n = 8), 150 (n = 10), every one proved, none needing H-bisection; smallest margins 2.05e-4, 1.25e-4, 2.70e-4.
3. **O2, the alternating pattern** (`o2n/`): Φ_M(θ, H) ≥ F_n(H) for all θ and H ∈ [H_lo(n), 2]. As in v0.2 this goes through the support-function dual Ψ_n (Lemma 2: inf_θ Φ_M = inf_r Ψ_n), the flat-orthant identity, the global deficit cap, clipping and the exact H = 2 case (Lemmas 5–11, generalised to every even n). v0.2's n = 6 corner certificate (a five-variable convex surrogate) does not scale; it is replaced by a block decomposition of Ψ_n with an analytic local lemma (§2). Referee reports: `docs/o2n-lemmas-review-codex-2026-11-01.md`, `docs/o2n-lemmas-review2-codex-2026-11-01.md` (analytic), `docs/o2n-review-codex-2026-11-01.md` (computation).

**Assembly.** O1's target is a chain value and O2's is F_n = min_T f_n; they are joined by O2 itself. On each o1n slice, Φ^σ > F_n_ub ≥ Φ_M(θ0, H) ≥ F_n(H), the last step by O2 at θ = θ0. On v0.2's range [1.87, 2) for n = 6, O1 gives Φ^σ > F6(H) = min_θ L(θ, H), which is the value of Φ_M at an equal-tilt vector and hence ≥ F_6(H) by the same O2. So every packing has L ≥ F_n(H) on R_n without using the identity f_n(cot θ; H) = L(θ, H) or F6 = F_6; those identities (checked to 1e-15, and f_n against Φ_M at 200 random points to 1e-10 in o2n gate G3) only identify F_n with the Morandi width. This joining step is written out here and not in a refereed file.

## 2. O2 by block decomposition

This is the part of the proof that is new relative to v0.2 and the part that should be reusable. All statements are in `o2n/LEMMAS.md`; lemma numbers refer to it.

*The dual and the flat orthant.* There is one dual variable r_j per gap, and Ψ_n(r; H) = m1(r_1) + m1(r_{n−1}) + Σ_{p=1}^{n−2} A(r_odd(p), r_even(p)) + Hσ(r), with m1 and A the one-angle minima of v0.2. The Morandi point is r\*(T) = (T, −1/T, …, −1/T, T); write ρ_j for r_j with the sign it has there, so the flat orthant is {ρ_j ≥ 0}. Each middle square contributes a pair p of an odd coordinate x_p = ρ_odd and an even one Y_p = ρ_even. On the flat orthant (Lemma 5)

    Ψ_n = n + (2 − H) Σ_j |r_j| − Σ_{n−2 pairs} d(x_p, Y_p),

and the pair deficit satisfies 0 ≤ d ≤ d0 with equality only at (1, 1) (Lemma 6). This gives F_n(2) = n − (n − 2)d0 for all r (Theorem 10), the tangent bound F_n(H) ≤ F_n(2) + (n − 1)(2 − H) (Lemma 7), clipping at −c(H), c(H) = √(4/H² − 1) (Lemma 9), and tail bounds (Lemma 20).

*Transfer multipliers.* The linear term k Σ|r_j| is distributed over the pairs: an interior coordinate lies in two pairs, which receive k/2 + μ_j and k/2 − μ_j. Whatever μ is chosen, the pair functions g_p(x, Y) = c_x x + c_Y Y − d(x, Y) sum exactly to Ψ_n − n (Lemma 13, telescoping). Require every g_p to be stationary along the ridge xY = 1 at the Morandi point: (Tan) c_x − c_Y/T² = D'(T). These are n − 2 equations in n − 3 unknowns; the first n − 3 are triangular and fix μ, and the last holds iff ∂_T f_n(T; H) = 0, i.e. T = T_n(H) (Lemma 18). The solution is explicit and antisymmetric, μ_{n−j} = −μ_j, with μ_{2i+1} = k(i/(m − 1) − 1/2) and μ_{2i} = (kT²/2)(m − 2i)/(m − 1); for n = 8, H = 1.9 it is μ = (+0.02896, −0.01667, 0, +0.01667, −0.02896).

*Even-star blocks.* Single pairs are not enough: every pair function vanishes at the origin, but with the stationary split some single pairs have positive value at the Morandi point, so the pure pair scheme fails below H ≈ 1.898–1.911 (LEMMAS.md §8, §13). The scheme that works groups the two pairs sharing each even coordinate 2j into one block B_j(x_l, Y, x_r), j = 1, …, m − 1, a function of three variables (Lemma 19). The even coordinate is internal to its block, so only the odd split matters, and that split is forced to be the one of Lemma 18 (Lemma 19(e)). At the Morandi point every block has the same value, B_j(T, 1/T, T) = 2(f_n(T; H) − n)/(n − 2), so 2(F_n − n)/(n − 2) at T = T_n(H), and the m − 1 blocks add up to F_n(H) − n exactly. The telescoping is an identity, so the decomposition loses nothing: Ψ_n ≥ F_n follows from B_j ≥ B_j(z\*) for each block separately (Lemma 14). The price is that each block obligation has zero margin at z\*, so interval enclosure alone cannot prove it; it is split into a local lemma near z\*, an origin lemma near 0, a tail bound, and a strict certified inequality on the rest. Blocks j and m − j are mirror images, so only j ≤ m/2 is computed. On wrong-sign coordinates a reversed split is used; with the orthant split a middle block dips below 0 next to the origin by about k²/(4(m − 1)²).

*Local tightness* (Lemma 16, pairs; Lemma 17, blocks). The deficit is d = max(0, dX, dY), where dX and dY coincide on the ridge xY = 1 and are affine transversally on each side of it (Lemma 15), so the only curvature that enters is that of the ridge function ℓ(u) = c_x u + c_Y/u − D(u). If c_x ≥ 0, (Tan) holds at T, and on a box N around (T, 1/T) one has (J) the projected ridge interval inside [1/2, 3/2], (N1), (N1') two transverse slope bounds against ψ(u) = 1 − 1/√(1 + u²), (N2') a corner bound on the linear part, (N3) c_Y ≥ 1 − min q on the x-range, and (L) ℓ'' > 0 on J, then g ≥ g(T, 1/T) on N. c_Y may be slightly negative: at the true parameters c_Y ∈ [0, 0.127], but on the cell ending at H = 2 the T-bracket forces T_lo < 1, where c_Y = −T_lo²D'(T_lo) ≈ −1.2e-5 at T_lo = 0.9999. The first version of the lemma required c_Y ≥ 0, which the implementer found unverifiable on that cell; (N3) and (L) replace it and hold there with slack about 0.4 and 0.048.

*Interval parameters* (Lemma 17'). T_n(H) is never computed exactly. On an H-cell the program finds rationals T_lo < T_hi with ∂_T f_n(T_lo; H) < 0 < ∂_T f_n(T_hi; H) at both endpoints; ∂_T f_n is affine in H, so the bracket holds on the whole cell. The hypotheses of Lemma 16 are then checked over the whole rectangle [T_lo, T_hi] × [H_a, H_b] with one fixed box N_cell containing (T, 1/T) for every T in the bracket. (Tan) is never checked with intervals; it holds exactly at the true (T_n(H), H), which lies in the rectangle. F_n is also never needed exactly, since F_n(H) ≤ f_n(T̂; H) for any trial T̂, and f_n(T̂; H) is affine in H.

*The origin* (Lemmas 21, 21'). As H decreases to H\*(n), the block value at z\* rises to 0, which is the block value at the origin, so the origin becomes a second near-minimiser. Lemma 21 gives B_j(z) ≥ κ0|z|_∞ − 4|z|²_∞ with κ0 = k/(m − 1), hence B_j ≥ 0 ≥ rhs on |z|_∞ ≤ κ0/4. At exactly H\*(n) any scheme that is exact in the sense of Lemma 14(c) has both z\* and 0 as minimisers of every block with the same value, so no strict certificate of this kind can reach H\*(n); this is the sliver [H\*(n), H_lo(n)) that stays open.

*Why n = 10 needs five-variable blocks below 1.86.* For n = 10 the even-star end block fails below H ≈ 1.84911 (grid evidence): at H\*(10) the minimum of B_1 − rhs is −2.6e-3, at x_1 = 0, Y_2 ≈ 0.667, x_3 ≈ 1.498, a configuration in which the end of the chain is half of a Morandi chain. Merging the first two even-star blocks into one block L in the five variables (x_1, Y_2, x_3, Y_4, x_5), with its mirror on the other side sharing x_5 (μ_5 = 0), removes the failure. Its origin lemma (Lemma 21') is B_L ≥ (k/2)|z|_∞ − 8|z|²_∞ and needs x_1 ≥ 0; the first referee found that the computational version of this obligation, as first written, was false without that restriction, and supplied the exact proof now used. The five-block is certified on [1.846936, 1.86] and the even-star blocks on [1.86, 2]. The same failure appears for n = 12 below 1.85268 (grid).

*Analytic versus computed.* Lemmas 1–21' and Theorem 22 (certified obligations imply O2_n on [H_lo(n), 2]) are proved in LEMMAS.md and were refereed twice, blind, by a GPT-class model (Codex CLI): the first pass had two MAJOR findings (the five-block origin obligation above, and the range not reaching H\*(n)), both repaired; the second had 0 MAJOR and 3 MINOR, all applied, verdict conditional yes on [H_lo(n), 2]. What the program certifies is a finite list per n and per H-cell: (H0) f_n(T̂; H_lo) < n and T_n ≥ T_0 on the range; (T) the bracket of T_n; (Loc) the hypotheses of Lemma 16 over the parameter rectangle; and (C) B_P > U at both cell endpoints on a cover of the capped domain minus N_cell minus the origin neighbourhood, using that B_P − U is affine in H for fixed z. The lower bounds reuse v0.2's A-evaluator verbatim, with one-dimensional inner minimisations over odd coordinates inside outer boxes in the even ones. Cells: 28 (n = 6), 30 (n = 8), 28 + 6 (n = 10, even-star plus five-block). Smallest margins: 1.1e-6, 1.5e-6, 6.6e-6 and 4.0e-8. The five-block range is the expensive one: 152,026 accepted outer boxes and 2,678 s of search, against at most 14,016 boxes and 75 s for each other range.

## 3. What is certified, and under what premises

| piece | statement | method | replay |
|---|---|---|---|
| reduction | L ≥ Φ^σ(θ, H) for some σ, any n, H < 2 | elementary, `docs/strip-n6-reduction.md` | none |
| O1, n = 6 | Φ^σ > F6 on [1.87, 2) | v0.2 certified DP, 76 slices | `cd o1dp && ../.venv/bin/python src/driver.py verify` |
| O1, n = 6 gap | Φ^σ > F_6_ub ≥ Φ_M(θ0) on [1.862, 1.87] | certified DP, 16 slices, interval-in-H target | `cd o1n && ../.venv/bin/python src/run_n6.py verify` |
| O1, n = 8, 10 | same on [1.855, 2), [1.850, 2) | certified DP, 145 and 150 slices | `cd o1n && ../.venv/bin/python src/driver.py verify --n 8` (resp. `--n 10`) |
| O2 analytic | (H0), (T), (Loc), (C) imply O2_n on [H_lo(n), 2] | `o2n/LEMMAS.md`, Theorem 22 | none |
| O2 computed | (H0), (T), (Loc), (C) for n = 6, 8, 10 | interval branch-and-bound per H-cell, C kernel | `cd o2n && ../.venv/bin/python src/driver.py verify --workers 6`, then `src/audit_bundle.py` |

Premises common to the computations: IEEE-754 binary64 with round-to-nearest basic operations, results widened outward with nextafter, a libm whose cos, sin and sqrt are within 8 ulps (padded by eight nextafter steps), and the pinned compiler settings (no fast-math; `-ffp-contract=off` for O2). These premises are not formally verified. The referees rebuilt the kernels with 64-step (O1) and 16-step (O2) padding on selected slices and boxes; margins changed by less than 7e-13 and 2e-14 respectively, which supports but does not replace the premise. Random gates, local-search witnesses and replay are reproducibility checks, not independent proofs of the interval code.

Referee record. O1 for n = 8, 10: verdict yes, no MAJOR or MINOR findings; the referee enumerated all role/cell paths on small grids, recomputed the targets at 80 digits and replayed both certificates. The n = 6 extension reuses the refereed driver and kernel unchanged (hashes match) through a new range runner; gates 1, 2, 5 and the lemma gate were not rerun for it; the kernel and target code are n-independent and unchanged, but this extension has had no blind referee pass of its own. O2 computation: verdict yes, 0 MAJOR, 3 MINOR, all about provenance or presentation (the shipped replay stopped at a stale LEMMAS.md hash after three wording corrections, an unrendered README line, a report generator that printed PASS unconditionally). The referee's replay, with that one hash exception, passed all 92 cells and 185,107 outer boxes with every margin reproduced exactly. The hash was rebound by `o2n/src/refresh_provenance.py` (old and new hash recorded in every manifest), after which the unmodified `driver.py verify` (92 cells, 185,107 outer boxes) and `audit_bundle.py` pass.

## 4. Not done

The theorem covers H ∈ R_n only. The slivers [H\*(6), 1.862), [H\*(8), 1.855) and [H\*(10), 1.850) are open. Below H_lo(n), O2 is open: at H\*(n) the blocks have two minimisers of equal value (Lemma 14(c)), so covering [H\*(n), H_lo(n)) (width under 1e-6) needs a root-constrained boundary argument. Above H_lo(n), O1 is open up to its lower end: at H\*(n) the all-floor row ties the target and the DP margin vanishes. The O1 lower ends were chosen, not forced; every slice closed without H-bisection, so ends closer to H\*(n) may be reachable; no run below these ends has been attempted. Nothing is claimed for H ≥ 2, where the pair constraint used in the reduction fails for stacked upright squares. There is no Lean formalisation; all four computations are conditional on the arithmetic premises of §3. n ≥ 12 is not attempted (larger blocks would be needed near H\*(12), §2); wand125's theorem covers n ≥ 11 from 1.851. The closed form of F_n and the uniqueness of the optimal tilt in the packing problem are not part of the proof.

## 5. Relation to other work, and lineage

- Prior results on strips, as checked on 31 October 2026 and again on 1 November 2026 in the public repositories evand/square-packing and wand125/square-packing: Evan Daniel proved the odd-n case for every odd n and H\*(9) ≤ H < 2 in Lean (`strip_odd_iff`). wand125's computer-assisted theorem (Hosono, "Minimum Widths for Unit Squares in Thin Rectangles", v3, 26 October 2026) gives the exact width for 1.851 ≤ H < 2 and every N ≥ 11, odd and even, by a different method, and states that smaller N are outside it. Neither covers n = 6, 8, 10. wand125's project page for the optimality of Friedman's regions S_n (wand125/square-packing issue 12) lists the curved piece of S_5, which is the n = 6 case here, as in progress by a weighted-point-set method, with its local step done, and S_6 to S_9 afterwards with the same tools. In that indexing the present n = 8 and n = 10 are the curved pieces of S_7 and S_9. The v0.2 note was announced on that issue on 1 November 2026; the present extension has not been announced.
- Morandi's configuration and the parametric width curve: Friedman's Math Magic, June 2010 (Maurizio Morandi). The formula L(θ, H) and the closed form of F6: `proof-F6.md` §3. The family F_{2m} is also in Daniel's `chainn/targets.py` and wand125's rectangle tables; here it enters only through f_n and Lemma 6.3.
- The Open Math swarm (workspace "The Square Packers", chats of 2026-10-27 to 10-31, coordinator Kimi K3): the vertex reduction idea, the dual form Ψ, and v0.2's corner certificate (member m1 of swarm smvuml416). v0.3 keeps the reduction and the dual form; the corner certificate is no longer used, though it remains an independent proof of the n = 6 O2 case on [1.87, 2] (`o2cert/`).
- The global deficit cap d ≤ 3 − 2√2 and the clipping argument: found by the Codex referee of route A (finding 14 of `docs/o2-routeA-review-codex-2026-10-31.md`), written out in `o2cert/LEMMAS.md` and generalised in `o2n/LEMMAS.md` §2–4.
- The block decomposition: a per-pair numerical study (`o2n/PAIRDECOMP-REPORT.md`, 2026-11-01, run by Claude at the author's direction) proposed the transfer multipliers and the end-triple scheme; three of its findings were corrected (`o2n/LEMMAS.md` §13). The even-star scheme, the reversed wrong-sign split, the local lemma in interval-parameter form and the five-block scheme are in `o2n/LEMMAS.md`. From the Codex referees: the existence proof of H\*(n) (Lemma 6.3(e)), the five-block origin bound (Lemma 21'), and the counterexample showing the normal-slope remark needs (L). From Codex during implementation: c_Y < 0 on the last H-cell (O2) and κ not even in dy (O1).
- O1's certified DP for general n, and O2's certified block computation: this repository (`o1n/`, `o2n/`), specifications by Kevin Fang, implementations by Codex CLI from those specifications, refereed blind by separate Codex runs.
