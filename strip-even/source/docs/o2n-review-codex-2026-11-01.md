2. **NOTE — Kernel provenance and the lower-bound arithmetic check out.** `cmp` confirms the entire 51-line `src/kernel.c` is byte-for-byte the current `o2cert/src/kernel.c`, including the candidate evaluator and centred bound.    In `kernel.c:9–19`, addition, subtraction, multiplication, reciprocal/division and squaring expand the appropriate ends with `nextafter`; square roots expand eight representable steps. Negation and absolute-value endpoint selection are exact. Domains are finite, small, and denominators positive. `candidate` includes the endpoint value and every available signed kink, with interval-enclosed kink direction; taking their minimum is the required lower bound. `alower` takes the **maximum** of two valid lower bounds, as does `block.c:51`: `return fmax(v.l,sub(pt(v0.l),loss).l)`. A minimum would remain safe but would be weaker; “min of plain and centred forms” is not the actual implementation.

   `block.c:32–40` implements the correct reversed coefficients on negative x and the positive coefficients on nonnegative x. `driver.py:114–121` splits a crossing range at zero; `block.c:43` requires each leaf to have one sign. The identity for negative x is still `A(x,-Y)-1-(1-c_tilde)x`, so the subtraction has the correct sign. The centre loss is safe: the support integrand's x-slope lies in `[0,sqrt(2)]`, or `[0,2sqrt(2)]` for the middle five-block term, and each Y slope has magnitude at most `sqrt(2)`. On these ranges the two endpoint magnitudes used for `dx` enclose the shifted slope interval. `radius` encloses the actual rounded centre-to-endpoint distances. The five-block middle term is exactly `A(x3,-Y2)+A(x3,-Y4)-2-H*x3`; `assemble` subtracts an interval enclosure of `H*Y`, or `H*(Y2+Y4)`, and takes the lower end. No tolerance is subtracted from a required inequality.

3. **NOTE — Affinity in H is used at the correct level.** For fixed spatial z, each coefficient `alpha`, `beta`, `alpha_tilde`, `beta_tilde`, `k/4`, `3k/4` or `k/2` is a constant multiple of `k=2-H`. Positive/negative parts and every A/deficit value depend only on z. No H-dependent coefficient enters a square root, minimization defining A, or other nonlinear operation in the mathematical block. Thus each triple and the five-block are affine in H. A single stored binary64 rational `T_hat` is used at both endpoints; `f_n(T_hat;H)` and hence the exact trial U are affine too. The *minimized lower-bound algorithm* need not be affine, and the proof does not require it. The same fixed cap domain and same exclusions are used for both endpoint evaluations; enlarging the negative-coordinate limits to the range-wide c cap is conservative.
