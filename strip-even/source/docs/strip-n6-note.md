# Six unit squares in a strip of height 1.87 ≤ H < 2: the minimum width is F6(H) (draft v0.2, 2026-10-31)

Status: draft v0.2, 2026-10-31; archived as https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23852984. Every mathematical step is either in the repository https://github.com/TheSnakeFang/squarepack with a blind referee report, or is a certified computation with a replay command; raw computational provenance is in https://github.com/TheSnakeFang/squarepack-certs (`strip-n6/`).

Notation: L(θ, H) = 2 + (4 − (3H − 2) cos θ)/sin θ + (4 − 2H sin θ)/cos θ for θ ∈ (0, π/2); F6(H) = min_θ L(θ, H). F6 is strictly decreasing, F6(1.87) = 5.956225297…, F6(2) = 8√2 − 6.

**Theorem.** For every H with 1.87 ≤ H < 2, every packing of six unit squares (arbitrary rotations, pairwise disjoint interiors) in a rectangle of width L and height H has L ≥ F6(H).

The bound is attained by Morandi's configuration: two upright squares in opposite corners and four squares of a common tilt θ*(H) between them, alternately on the floor and the ceiling. It is the parametric boundary piece for six squares in Erich Friedman's Math Magic problem of June 2010 (https://erich-friedman.github.io/mathmagic/0610.html, the curve in the row n = 5, credited there to Maurizio Morandi), with parameter t = sin θ*; that curve agrees with (F6(H), H) to 10⁻¹¹ at H = 1.87, 1.90, 1.95, 1.99, and the identity L(arcsin t, a(t)) = b(t) is the symbolic check (A) of `runs/openmath-2026-10-27/proof-F6.md` §3.2. Its validity as a packing of width L(θ*(H), H) = F6(H) was checked there in exact rational arithmetic (§3.1), so F6(H) is the minimum width on this range. The closed form F6(H) = b(a^{-1}(H)) is not used in the proof.

## 1. Structure of the proof

1. **Reduction to chain functions** (`docs/strip-n6-reduction.md`, refereed: `docs/strip-n6-reduction-review-codex-2026-10-31.md`). Sort the centres by x. For consecutive squares, disjoint interiors force the centre advance to be at least κ(θ_i, θ_{i+1}, Y_{i+1} − Y_i), the right boundary of the Minkowski sum of the two centred squares at the height difference; this needs |Y_{i+1} − Y_i| < 1, which H < 2 supplies. Summing gives L ≥ h(θ_1) + h(θ_6) + Σ κ. For fixed tilts this is concave in the centre heights, so its minimum over the height box is at a vertex: every square on the floor (F) or the ceiling (C). Hence L ≥ Φ^σ(θ, H) for the packing's tilts θ and some role pattern σ ∈ {F, C}^6.
2. **O1, the 62 non-alternating patterns** (`o1dp/`, refereed: `docs/o1dp-review-codex-2026-10-30.md`): Φ^σ(θ, H) > F6(H) for all θ, all H ∈ [1.87, 2), all σ ∉ {FCFCFC, CFCFCF}. A certified min-plus dynamic program over tilt cells and 76 H-slices; three interval lower bounds per gap (plain, correlated ratio, near-upright pair lemma), outward rounding in C; smallest slice margin 0.0095.
3. **O2, the alternating pattern** (`o2cert/`, refereed: `docs/o2cert-review-codex-2026-10-31.md`): Φ_M(θ, H) ≥ F6(H) for all θ and all H ∈ [1.87, 2]. The mirror pattern CFCFCF reduces to FCFCFC by reflecting the strip. The proof has three parts:
   - *Dual form.* κ is a support-function infimum over one real parameter per gap, so inf_θ Φ_M = inf_r Ψ(r; H) with Ψ(r; H) = m1(r1) + m1(r5) + A(r1, r2) + A(r3, r2) + A(r3, r4) + A(r5, r4) + Hσ(r), σ = −r1 + r2 − r3 + r4 − r5, where m1 and A are explicit minima over one angle with finite candidate sets (`o2cert/LEMMAS.md` Lemmas 1–3). Equality Ψ = F6 holds at r*(H) = (T, −1/T, T, −1/T, T), T = tan(π/2 − θ*(H)).
   - *Analytic reduction to a box.* On the orthant r1, r3, r5 ≥ 0 ≥ r2, r4 one has Ψ = 6 + (2 − H)(−σ) − Σ d over the four pairs, where the pair deficit satisfies 0 ≤ d ≤ 3 − 2√2 everywhere (Lemma 6; equality exactly at the Morandi point). Since 6 − 4(3 − 2√2) = F6(2) and F6(H) ≤ L(π/4, H) = F6(2) + 5(2 − H), a clipping lemma (Lemma 9) and the exact H = 2 case (Theorem 10) reduce Ψ ≥ F6 to the compact box K = [0, 6.14] × [−6.14, 0.38] × [−0.38, 6.14] × [−6.14, 0.38] × [0, 6.14] (Theorem 11).
   - *Two certificates on K.* Near r*(H) (sup-norm radius 0.32, all H): the convex-surrogate certificate of the Open Math swarm (nine interval scans over 52 H-cells, 414,652 boxes, margins ≥ 0.02), replayed with directed rounding in `o2cert/src/corner_impl`. On the rest of K: a branch-and-bound in the two ceiling coordinates with certified one-dimensional inner minima, an affine-in-H endpoint test against the trial width L(θ0, H) ≥ F6(H), outward rounding in C; 8 H-cells, 186,155 outer rectangles (182,455 numerical, 3,700 closed analytically by the H = 2 bound), every rectangle re-checked by replay.

## 2. What is certified, and under what premises

| piece | statement | method | replay |
|---|---|---|---|
| reduction | L ≥ Φ^σ(θ, H) for some σ | elementary, `docs/strip-n6-reduction.md` | — |
| O1 | Φ^σ > F6 on [1.87, 2), 62 patterns | certified DP, 76 slices, C kernel | `cd o1dp && ../.venv/bin/python src/driver.py verify` |
| O2 corner | Ψ ≥ F6 on |r − r*(H)|_∞ ≤ 0.32 | nine interval scans (swarm m1), directed replay | `cd o2cert && ../.venv/bin/python src/corner_replay.py` |
| O2 box | Ψ > L(θ0, H) ≥ F6 on K \ corner | 2-D branch-and-bound, 1-D inner minima, C kernel | `cd o2cert && gunzip -k certificate.json.gz && ../.venv/bin/python src/driver.py verify` |

Premises common to the computations: IEEE-754 binary64 arithmetic with outward rounding, and a libm whose cos, sin and sqrt are within the stated padding (8 ulps in O2; see `o1dp/SPEC.md` for O1). The corner certificate's symbolic identities (the surrogate's value and stationarity at r*) are proved in the swarm's `PROOF.md` and were re-derived by the referee of O2; its typographical slips are listed in `o2cert/README.md`.

The three referee reports were produced blind by a second model (Codex CLI) from a packet that named the claims and asked for re-derivation; each report's findings were applied and the certificates replayed afterwards. No report found a counterexample; the reduction's first draft had one real error (a false interiority claim), repaired as the review records.

## 3. Not done

The theorem covers 1.87 ≤ H < 2 only. The lower end 1.87 was inherited from the problem as posed to the Open Math swarm, which took it from the range of Evan Daniel's odd-n strip theorem at the time (22 October 2026); it enters O1 through the H-slices and O2 through the clipping lemma (Lemma 9, H ≥ 1.87). The natural lower end is H*(6) = 1.86110…, the height at which F6(H) = 6: below it the row of six upright squares has width 6 < F6(H), so the exact answer there is min(6, F6(H)) and the statement L ≥ F6(H) is false. The interval [H*(6), 1.87) is therefore open; closing it would mean rerunning O1 and O2 with the lemmas rechecked at the lower bound. Nothing is claimed for H ≥ 2, where the pair constraint used in the reduction fails for stacked upright squares. The closed form of F6 and the uniqueness of the optimal tilt are not part of the proof.

## 4. Relation to other work, and lineage

- Prior results on strips, as of 31 October 2026 (checked in the public repositories evand/square-packing and wand125/square-packing): Evan Daniel proved the odd-n case for every odd n and H*(9) ≤ H < 2 in Lean (`strip_odd_iff`); wand125's computer-assisted theorem ("Minimum Widths for Unit Squares in Thin Rectangles", v3, 26 October 2026) gives the exact width for 1.851 ≤ H < 2 and every N ≥ 11, odd and even, and states that smaller N are outside it. Neither covers n = 6. wand125's project page for the optimality of Friedman's regions S_n (wand125/square-packing issue 12, updated 22 October 2026) lists the curved piece of S_5, which is this theorem, as in progress by a weighted-point-set method, with its local step done; as of 31 October we know of no completed proof of the six-square case.
- Morandi's configuration and the parametric width curve: Friedman's Math Magic, June 2010 (Maurizio Morandi). The formula L(θ, H) and the closed form of F6: `proof-F6.md` §3; the same family (F_{2m}) is in Daniel's `chainn/targets.py` and wand125's rectangle tables.
- The Open Math swarm (workspace "The Square Packers", chats of 2026-10-27 to 10-31, coordinator Kimi K3): the vertex reduction idea, the dual form Ψ, the corner certificate (member m1 of swarm smvuml416), the U-shell and tail computations that guided but do not enter the final certificate, and the drafts in `runs/openmath-2026-10-27/`. Their route A package and its blind review are `docs/o2-routeA-review-codex-2026-10-31.md`.
- The global deficit cap d ≤ 3 − 2√2 and the clipping argument: found by the Codex referee of route A (finding 14 of that report), written out in `o2cert/LEMMAS.md`.
- O1's certified DP and O2's box certificate: this repository (`o1dp/`, `o2cert/`), specifications by Kevin Fang, implementations by Codex CLI from those specifications, refereed blind by a second Codex run.
