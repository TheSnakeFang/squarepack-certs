# O2 certified computation for even n ∈ {6, 8, 10}: the block obligations of `LEMMAS.md` §11

Goal: certify the inequalities (H0), (T), (Loc) and (C) listed in `LEMMAS.md` §11 (revised 2026-11-01 after the blind review `../docs/o2n-lemmas-review-codex-2026-11-01.md`), with the mechanics of §10, so that Theorem 22 gives O2_n on [H_lo(n), 2] with the rational H_lo(6) = 1.861098, H_lo(8) = 1.852049, H_lo(10) = 1.846936. The interval [H*(n), H_lo(n)) is not covered and is not claimed. There is no computational origin obligation: Lemma 21' gives κ0^L = k/2 exactly. Everything mathematical is fixed by `LEMMAS.md`; do not change it. If something looks wrong, stop and say so in the README under "Issues with the spec" (two earlier attempts did exactly that, `README-attempt1.md`, `README-attempt2.md`; both issues are now fixed in LEMMAS.md and here). Model the code on `../o2cert/src` (C kernel with directed rounding, Python driver with modes `gates`, `run`, `verify`, `report`, loud gates). Read `../o2cert/SPEC.md` first: its "Certified lower bounds" section (candidate evaluation of A on a box, centred form, Lipschitz bound √2), its H handling and its gate style all carry over.

## Objects (from LEMMAS.md)

- k = 2 − H, c(H) = √(4/H² − 1) rounded up, d0 = 3 − 2√2, w = u + 1/u, P = √(w(w + 2)), D(u) = 1 + w − P, D'(u) = (1 − 1/u²)(1 − (w + 1)/P), D''(u) = (1 − u⁻²)²/P³ − 2/(u³ P (P + w + 1)) (as in LEMMAS.md; verify the formula symbolically or numerically in a gate before use), ψ(u) = 1 − 1/√(1 + u²), q(u) = √(1 + 2/w).
- f_n(T; H) = n + k(mT + (m − 1)/T) − (n − 2)D(T), ∂_T f_n = k(m − (m − 1)/T²) − (n − 2)D'(T), m = n/2. T_n(H) is the zero of ∂_T f_n in [1/2, 3/2]; F_n(H) = f_n(T_n(H); H).
- A(x, y) by the candidate set of `o2cert` Lemma 3 (`A_lb` on boxes exactly as in `../o2cert/SPEC.md`, with the centred form).
- s(x, Y; c, c̃) = A(x, −Y) − 1 − (1 − c)x⁺ + (1 − c̃)x⁻. For x ≥ 0 this is A(x, −Y) − 1 − (1 − c)x; for x < 0 it is A(x, −Y) − 1 − (1 − c̃)x. A lower bound on an x-box that contains 0 is the min of the two sign pieces, each evaluated on its part.
- Triple block (even-star block j): B_j(x_l, Y, x_r) = s(x_l, Y; β, β̃) + s(x_r, Y; α, α̃) − (2 − k)Y, with the coefficients of §11 per n and j. Domain D_j: Y ∈ [−c, ∞), x_l, x_r ∈ [−c, ∞) except that an end coordinate (x_1, x_{n−1}) is ∈ [0, ∞).
- Five-variable block L (n = 10 only): B_L as in §11 with variables (x_1, Y_2, x_3, Y_4, x_5), x_1 ≥ 0.
- rhs upper bound: U(H) := 2(f_n(T̂; H) − n)/(n − 2) for the triple blocks and U_L(H) := (f_10(T̂; H) − 10)/2 for the 5-block, evaluated with upward rounding, with one rational T̂ per cell (the same T̂ at both endpoints), near T_n(H_mid). U ≥ rhs for every H (Lemma 6.3(c),(d)), and U is affine in H.
- Caps on c(H) over the ranges: 0.3935 (n = 6), 0.4077 (n = 8), 0.3953 (n = 10 even-star, H ≥ 1.86), 0.4155 (n = 10 five-block). Coordinate caps (tails) are the ones printed in §11, proved by Lemma 20(c) for every H < 2; they are not recomputed per cell.

## Per H-cell checks

H-cells cover [H_lo(n), 2] with endpoints stored as binary64 rounded outward (first lower endpoint ≤ H_lo rounded down from the rational H_lo; last upper endpoint = 2). Start with cells of width 0.005 and bisect a cell that does not close. For each cell [H_a, H_b] and each block to certify (§11 says which; mirror blocks are not run):

1. (T) Bracket T_n on the cell: find rationals T_lo < T_hi with T_hi − T_lo ≤ 1/4 (1e-3 is typical) and, with outward rounding, the upper bound of ∂_T f_n(T_lo; H_e) < 0 and the lower bound of ∂_T f_n(T_hi; H_e) > 0 at both H_e = H_a and H_b (∂_T f_n is affine in H for fixed T, so the signs then hold on the whole cell). On a cell ending at 2 this forces T_lo < 1 < T_hi, which is expected. Log T_lo, T_hi.
2. (Loc) Local hypotheses, Lemma 17' with the revised Lemma 16: the fixed box N_cell = [T_hi − 1/4, T_lo + 1/4] × [1/T_lo − 1/4, 1/T_hi + 1/4] × [T_hi − 1/4, T_lo + 1/4] (x_l, Y, x_r; rounded inward so that N_cell is a subset of the true neighbourhood); check N_cell is nonempty and contains (T, 1/T, T) for every T in the bracket. Over the whole parameter rectangle (T, H) ∈ [T_lo, T_hi] × [H_a, H_b], for both pairs of the block with c_x = β (resp. α) and c_Y = T²(c_x − D'(T)) as an interval in (T, H), check (J), (N1), (N1'), (N2'), (N3) and (L) of the revised Lemma 16, where (L) is c_Y > sup_{u ∈ J} u³ D''(u)/2 checked by subdividing J. c_Y may be slightly negative near H = 2; that is allowed by the revised lemma. Do NOT check γ ≥ 0 and never check (Tan). For the 5-block, the same for its four pairs, with c_x = k, k/4, 3k/4, k/2 and N_cell^5 the product box with the same factors. Log each hypothesis's minimum slack.
3. Origin neighbourhood N_0 = {|z|_∞ ≤ κ0/4} with κ0 = k(H_b)/(m − 1) rounded down (Lemma 21(c)); for the 5-block, N_0^L = {|z|_∞ ≤ k(H_b)/16} (Lemma 21'). At H_b = 2 nothing is excluded. N_0 is only an exclusion when U(H_a) > −κ0²/16; otherwise the origin region is certified by (C) like any other region.
4. (C) on the rest of the domain. Certify, for both endpoints H_e ∈ {H_a, H_b}:
       B_lb(box; H_e) > U(H_e)   (left side rounded down, right side rounded up),
   on a cover by boxes of the §11 domain (coordinate ranges from the c cap and the §11 coordinate caps; the 5-block tail is the sum-of-positive-parts form of §11) minus N_cell minus N_0. Justification (write in a comment): B(z; H) − U(H) is affine in H for fixed z (B depends on H only through k, affinely, Lemma 19(c); U is affine), so positivity at both endpoints gives positivity on the cell; the domain uses the c cap so it covers the cell.
   Organisation for a triple block: outer boxes are Y-intervals (one dimension); for each outer box, B_lb = s_lb(x_l over its full range, Y-box) + s_lb(x_r over its full range, Y-box) − (2 − k)·Y (interval, rounded down), where each s_lb is a certified lower bound of min over x of s(x, Y; ·) with Y an interval, computed by a 1-D best-first branch-and-bound over x bounded by max(plain, centred). Exclusions: N_cell is a product, so a point is excluded only if all three coordinates are in their factors; if the Y-box is inside N_cell's Y-factor, the inner problem is min over (x_l, x_r) ∉ I_x × I_x = union of {x_l ∉ I_x} and {x_r ∉ I_x}; certify each separately (the x_l pieces [range.lo, I_x.lo] and [I_x.hi, range.hi] against x_r over its full range, and vice versa). Same for N_0 with its three factors. A Y-box straddling a factor boundary is split until inside or outside, unless it certifies without exclusion first. Best-first bisection of Y-boxes; a Y-box is certified when the inequality holds at both endpoints for all its pieces.
   For the 5-block: outer boxes are (Y_2, Y_4) rectangles; inner one-variable minimisations of s(x_1, Y_2; k, ·) over x_1 ≥ 0, of A(x_3, −Y_2) + A(x_3, −Y_4) − 2 − (2 − k)x_3 over x_3 ∈ [−c, cap] (with both Y intervals), and of s(x_5, Y_4; k/2, k/2) over x_5; exclusions N_cell^5 (five factors; the inner union has three pieces, one per odd coordinate) and N_0^L.
5. (H0), once per n: f_n(T̂; H_lo) < n with upward rounding, T̂ rational; and ∂_T f_n(T₀; H_lo) < 0 (upper bound) at T₀ = 0.887 (n = 6), 0.919 (n = 8), 0.936 (n = 10), which with (T) gives T_n ≥ T₀ on the range.

## Gates (random, zero violations; log to `gates.log` per n)

- G1: A_lb and the centred form vs a dense-sampling reference, as `../o2cert/SPEC.md` G1–G2 (reuse that code; it is already refereed). Also D, D', D'' formulas against mpmath differentiation at 1000 random u ∈ [1/2, 3/2] (1e-10).
- G2 (block soundness): 20 000 random (Y-box, x ranges, H_e): the certified B_lb ≤ B_j at 20 random points of the box (B_j by the reference A); same for the 5-block with (Y_2, Y_4)-boxes.
- G3 (identities): f_n(T; H) equals Ψ_n(r*(T); H) computed from the reference A at 200 random (n, T, H) to 1e-10; B_j(T, 1/T, T) = 2(f_n(T; H) − n)/(n − 2) at 200 random (n, j, T, H) to 1e-12; the telescoping identity Σ_j B_j = Ψ_n − n at 500 random r with ρ_1, ρ_{n−1} ≥ 0 (and random signs elsewhere) to 1e-10; B_L = B_1 + B_2 for n = 10 at 500 random points.
- G4 (T bracketing): at 300 random H per n, the certified bracket contains the T found by a high-precision root finder (mpmath), and f_n(T̂; H) ≥ F_n(H).
- G5 (local lemma sanity): at 2 000 random (n, H, z ∈ N_cell), B_j(z) ≥ rhs(H) − 1e-12 with the reference A; include cells ending at 2 where c_Y < 0, and separately 2 000 random pair instances (T, c_x, c_Y with c_Y ∈ [−2e-3, 0]) satisfying the revised Lemma 16 hypotheses, checking g ≥ ℓ(T) on the box.
- G6 (origin): at 2 000 random z with |z|_∞ ≤ κ0/4 in D_j, B_j(z) ≥ 0; for the 5-block, 2 000 random z with |z|_∞ ≤ k/16 and x_1 ≥ 0, B_L(z) ≥ 0, and 2 000 random z on the cone x_1 ≥ 0 with |z|_∞ = 1, B1_L(z) ≥ (k/2) − 1e-12.
